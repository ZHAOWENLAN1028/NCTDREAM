First, what is NFC

A very short answer

NFC is a small wireless chip. When my phone is near the card, the phone can read the card and open the music. There is no CD in this version, so the card is the "disc".

Steps I use

Four steps

  1. Turn on NFC in the phone settings.
  2. Open the album app, or install it if the phone asks.
  3. Put the phone on the top of the card and hold it still for two seconds.
  4. Wait for the app to open, then press play.

Problems I met

What helped

  • The phone did not read the card at first. I moved it to the top part of the card and it worked.
  • A thick phone case can block the signal, so I take the case off.
  • The app was slow on the campus Wi-Fi, so I opened it once at home and it was fine later.

Why this is a digital service

Small thought

The album is still a physical thing, but the music comes through a chip and an app. So the company sells a product and a service at the same time, and the user needs a phone to finish the experience.

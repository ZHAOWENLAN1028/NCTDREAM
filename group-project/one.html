The package

Outside

The case is clear plastic with a hanger hole on top, so it looks a little like a small toy pack. On the front there is a green SMini label in the corner and the album title in white.

Size

It is about the size of a phone, so it fits in a small bag and is easy to carry to class.

What is in the case

Four things

Item What it is
Clear caseThe plastic pack with the cover photo
NFC music cardA small card with the album music on it
PhotocardOne member photo card, it comes out easily
Lyric cardA card with the song words printed on it

How I take care of it

Three small rules

  • I keep the case in a dry place, because the plastic can get scratches.
  • I do not bend the photocard, so it stays flat for the shelf.
  • I put the NFC card back after I use it, so I do not lose it.

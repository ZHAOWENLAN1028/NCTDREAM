About the album

What it is

DREAM AFTER DREAM is an NCT DREAM album. I bought the SMini version, which is the small smart album, not the usual big box with a CD.

How it looks

It comes in a clear plastic case with a hanger hole, like a small keyring pack. The cover photo is green grass and trees, and the album title is printed in big white letters.

Why I like this version

Three reasons

  • It is small, so I can put it on my bag or my shelf.
  • It has a photocard inside, and the card is easy to take out and look at.
  • The music is on a small NFC card, so I can play it with my phone.
